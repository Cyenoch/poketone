# 美术提示词模板

生成 2D 素材（卡面、图标、贴图）时复用这里的风格段，保证画风一致。每次生成后把实际提示词、模型和输出路径追加到「生成记录」，并在素材旁写 provenance。

工具：Magpie Image（`generate_image`），默认模型以 Magpie 设置为准。3D 模型走 Tripo，规则见 [开发手册](DEVELOPMENT.md)「3D 模型与动画（Tripo + Blender）」。

## 风格段（必须原样附在每个提示词末尾）

> Bright clean cartoon game art for a low-poly Pokémon-style adventure. Chunky simple shapes, flat cel shading with two or three tones, soft rim light, thick rounded dark navy (#142f50) outline. Saturated but not neon colours, friendly and readable at 48 px. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no borders, no drop shadows outside the subject.

## 图标表模板

一次生成整张网格再本地切片，比逐张生成省调用，也更统一：

> A sprite sheet of {N} game icons arranged in a perfectly even {cols}×{rows} grid of equal square cells separated by thin uniform gaps, each icon centered in its cell on a plain flat warm off-white (#f5f1e8) background. Row by row, left to right: {逐格描述}. {风格段}

切片：`python3 assets/icons/slice-sheet.py <sheet.png> <cols> <rows> <输出目录> <名字...>`。切完逐张目视检查，错格或缺格重新生成整张。

## 地面贴图模板

> Seamless tileable top-down ground texture for a bright cartoon low-poly forest game: {地表描述}, painterly flat shading, low detail, no strong shadows, no perspective, no text. Must tile seamlessly on all four edges. Even lighting, readable as a neutral backdrop under characters.

## 侧视贴图模板（崖壁、土坡）

> Seamless tileable texture for a bright cartoon low-poly forest game: {侧视描述，如 a steep earthen cliff bank seen straight on from the side, horizontal strata …}, soft cel-shaded chunky planes, no grass, no moss, no sky, no landmarks that would reveal repetition, painterly flat shading, low detail, no strong cast shadows, no perspective, no text. Must tile seamlessly on all four edges. Even lighting.

着色器按世界坐标侧投影（X / Z 两面），贴图的竖直方向对应世界 Y，所以地层要画成水平条带。

## 卡面底纹模板（选卡浮层）

底纹铺在卡面文字下面，所以风格段里「thick navy outline」一句换成浅色描边，其余照抄：

> A decorative full-bleed background pattern for a game choice card: {图案}. Light airy palette dominated by {卡面色名} ({卡面色 hex}) with gentle {辅色} accents, soft low-contrast tonal shapes, motifs clustered toward the edges and corners with a calm, nearly empty lighter centre, fills the whole square edge to edge, no frame. Thin soft outlines in a slightly darker tint of the same colour, no dark navy or black lines. Bright clean cartoon game art for a low-poly Pokémon-style adventure. Chunky simple shapes, flat cel shading with two or three tones. Saturated but not neon colours. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no borders, no drop shadows.

1024×1024 单张生成（四周有图案、中间留空，`cover` 裁成竖卡或紧凑档横条都能看到边缘图案）。处理：缩到 512px，向卡面色（`--type-soft`，道具 #f3e3c3，训练 #e3e8ee）混合，逐档减少原图占比直到每个像素对 ink #142f50 ≥4.8:1，再存 WebP（quality 86）。

> Classic low-polygon video-game asset, economical silhouette geometry, simple clean diffuse color regions, few broad forms, no tiny surface details, not realistic, not crystalline faceted, not plush. Full body isolated, no pedestal, no scenery, no text.

生成后看 `preview.png`（纹理是否为空），`bun run assets:game` 减面到约 900 面，再用 Blender 从 +X / +Z 渲染确认朝向、登记 `facingYaw`。
