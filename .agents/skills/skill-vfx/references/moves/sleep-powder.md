# 催眠粉 Sleep Powder (`sleep-powder`) — grass · cone · big (utility)

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/催眠粉> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sleep_Powder_(move)>
- Description: 撒出催眠粉，使对手陷入睡眠状态。 *[52poke]*

Signature traits
- Powder **scattered** from the bulb/flower, enveloping the target. *[52poke]*
- Most often **sparkling blue** powder (Bulbasaur, Victreebel…); colour varies by user
  (pale pink, green, yellow). *[52poke]*

Reference game: pixel spores in three pastel colours (mint, lavender, light blue), ~0.28 → 0.55
size scaled by √(area/7), drifting forward with slight gravity, life ≈ 1.5× the area life; a rare
mint twinkle.

Sim: cone length 1.9, half-angle 0.55, life 1.4 s, ticks 0.2 s, follows the player; 1.5 dmg —
it is a status move.

Colour conflict: saturated blue = XP candy, saturated purple = enemy poison spores. Use a pale,
desaturated pastel (whitish blue/lilac) cloud with white sparkles, slow drift (< 60 BPM pulse).
Avoid: opaque fills, ink rings, purple globs.
