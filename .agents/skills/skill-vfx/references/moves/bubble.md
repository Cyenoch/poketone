# 泡沫 Bubble (`bubble`) — water · barrage · basic

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/泡沫> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bubble_(move)>
- Description (paraphrased): 将无数泡泡吹向对手进行攻击，有时会降低对手速度。 *[familiar]*

Signature traits
- **Many** bubbles blown from the mouth toward the target. *[52poke]*
- Slow, drifting, bobbing flight; pops on contact. *[familiar]*

Reference game: white bubble with two small satellite bubbles orbiting and bobbing, a twinkle
highlight; trail of tiny rising bubbles; hit = quick splash (0.18 s), drops and four bubbles.

Sim: 4 shots, radius 0.25, speed 9 (slow), dur 1.6 s, 70 ms stagger.

Keep: transparent rimmed bubble with highlight, pop. Avoid: opaque blue balls.
