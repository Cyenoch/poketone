# 电球 Electro Ball (`electro-ball`) — electric · orb · mid

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/电球> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Electro_Ball_(move)>
- Description: 用电气团撞向对手。自己比对手速度越快，威力越大。 *[52poke]*

Signature traits
- A **ball of electricity** crackling with sparks. *[52poke]*
- Gathered in front of the body (in the anime, Pikachu forms it at its tail tip) and hurled. *[52poke / tail: familiar]*

Reference game: soft volt glow 1.9× + rotating bolt-shaped core; spark trail (0.2 s); boom =
ground ring at the radius, four lightning arcs out to the radius, bolt-spark burst.

Sim: orb head radius 0.3, speed 16, dur 1.2 s; burst radius 1, life 0.3 s.

Keep: crackling sphere, radial arcs to exactly the burst radius. Avoid: glow decal under the
flying ball larger than the head's reach.
