# 火花 Ember (`ember`) — fire · bolt · basic

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/火花> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ember_(move)>
- Description: 向对手发射小型火焰进行攻击。有时会让对手陷入灼伤状态。 *[52poke]*

Signature traits
- A **small** flame/fireball launched at one target — clearly smaller than Flamethrower. *[52poke]*
- Spat from the mouth (Charmander), often as a few little embers in a row. *[familiar — check the GIF]*

Reference game: head = pixel flame sprite with a soft fire glow ≈ 1.3× its size; trail of tiny
square embers with gravity (≈ 0.35 s); hit = four small flame puffs and a small smoke puff.

Sim: speed 20, dur 0.9 s, radius 0.22, pierce 0, cd 0.9 s (basic tier).

Keep: tiny bright head, short ember trail, small hit puff. Avoid: big halos, ground decals in
flight, scorch bigger than the head's hit.
