# 飞叶快刀 Razor Leaf (`razor-leaf`) — grass · barrage · basic

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/飞叶快刀> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Razor_Leaf_(move)>
- Description: 飞出叶片，切斩对手。容易击中要害。 *[52poke]*

Signature traits
- **Sharp, thin leaves**, often glowing green, fired from the back/bulb. *[52poke]*
- **Several** at once, spinning. *[52poke]* *[spin: familiar]*

Reference game: leaf sprite ≈ 1.2× size, leaf trail half the time, hit = small cross flash +
four leaf bits.

Sim: 4 shots, radius 0.2, speed 17, dur 1 s, pierce 1, 70 ms stagger, ±0.16 rad spread (each ±0.05 jitter).

Keep: spinning leaf with a short pale streak. Avoid: big streaks; anything louder than a basic.
