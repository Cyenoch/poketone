# 打雷 Thunder (`thunder`) — electric · smite · big

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/打雷> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Thunder_(move)>
- Description: 向对手劈下暴雷进行攻击。有时会让对手陷入麻痹状态。 *[52poke]*

Signature traits
- A massive bolt **from the sky** onto the target. *[52poke]*
- Often from a dark cloud; a blinding flash. *[familiar]*

Reference game: bolt from far above (≈ 32 m) with a white core bolt, three side bolts from
8–18 m up to the ground at 1.4× radius, flash at 1.2× radius for 0.12 s, sparks, ground cracks at
1.3× radius for 1.2 s, small camera shake.

Sim: 2 strikes at targets within 13 m, radius 0.9, delay = dur 0.55 s (telegraph), life 0.8 s.

Keep: player-coloured ground telegraph filling to the strike, thin tall bolt, cracks residue.
Avoid: telegraph rim looking like an enemy warning (no red, no chevrons), cracks wider than ~1.1 r.
