# 大字爆炎 Fire Blast (`fire-blast`) — fire · orb · big

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/大字爆炎> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fire_Blast_(move)>
- Description: 用大字形状的火焰烧尽对手。有时会让对手陷入灼伤状态。 *[52poke]*

Signature traits
- The flame takes the shape of the kanji **大** (a five-armed figure) — its defining look. *[52poke]*
- The blast surrounds the target; a small fireball forms first, then bursts. *[52poke]*
- Variants: X or star shapes (Gyarados, Salamence). *[52poke]*

Reference game: head = big soft deep-fire glow (1.5×) + flame sprite; boom = ground ring at the
radius, a 0.12 s flash at 0.7 radius, **five arms of five flame puffs** launched outward in 35 ms
steps (a star/大 spread), a central column of flames, smoke, two delayed smoke puffs at
0.2–0.28 s, and a dark scorch at 1.6× radius for 1.5 s.

Sim: orb head radius 0.3, speed 13, dur 1.4 s; bursts (radius 1.25, life 0.3 s) on first hit or at
end of range.

Keep: 大 shape revealed fast on the ground and in flame, scorch 大 residue. Avoid: a ring larger
than the burst radius, long-lived bright decals.
