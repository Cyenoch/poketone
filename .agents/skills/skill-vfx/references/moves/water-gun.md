# 水枪 Water Gun (`water-gun`) — water · bolt · basic

- 52poke: <https://wiki.52poke.com/wiki/水枪> · Bulbapedia: <https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Water_Gun_(move)>
- Description: 向对手猛烈地喷射水流进行攻击。 *[52poke]*

Signature traits
- A forceful **jet/stream** of water at one target, not a single droplet. *[52poke]*

Reference game: head = a chain of four water balls trailing back and shrinking, wobbling, with
a foam highlight; trail drops with heavy gravity; hit = small splash.

Sim: speed 22, dur 0.85 s, radius 0.22, pierce 1.

Keep: elongated jet (head + short segmented tail), splash on hit. Avoid: light-cyan round blobs
lingering near the ground (candy).
