# 催眠术 · hypnosis

- Source: [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hypnosis_(move)). Sleep induced by hypnotic suggestion. Pokémon identity: concentric psychic waves / turning hypnotic spirals; no downloaded reference art. The linked game's animation has not been inspected frame by frame.
- Keep: a spinning, inked pink/pale hypnotic spiral disc in front of the caster and nested pink/lilac hoops rolling out of it in rhythm. No spores, fog fill, ground-only texture, confetti, white cast star or enemy dashed borders.
- Sim truth: `cone`, base length 3, half-angle `CONE.angle` .55, life 1.2 s, tick .2, no base delay. The origin follows the interpolated owner; aim stays fixed for that cast. Damage already covers the whole fan on the first tick. Sleep chance is real simulation state, not a visual guarantee.
- Intent / tier: readable mid-tier control; an outward hypnotic rhythm with the enemy still visible through its hollow centre.
- Cast: the spiral disc (`Shape.spiral`, ~.24 m half-size, 25 cm above `CHEST`) pops in with a small overshoot over 140 ms while the fan is already damaging. Delayed instances only grow the disc at the caster until the actual damage-ready frame; no invented gameplay wind-up.
- Active: one hoop leaves the disc on every damage tick (phase-locked to `tickTimer`) and the disc kicks as it does. Each hoop is a horizontal circle open at the back (74% of a turn) that eases out over 600 ms from the disc down to chest height, growing to fill the live fan: centre c and radius r = c·sin(angle)·.92 stay inside the cone and c + r inside `length`. Successive hoops nest as "(((" instead of crossing; pink and lilac alternate.
- Contact: damage numbers and the status system's body tint / attached Z marks. Hypnosis does not restart the enemy white-flash timer or emit generic white/gold contact sparks; hit/contact events carry their source move for the same treatment on host and owner. An existing white flash from another move may finish normally. Damage, crit rolls and actual sleep application keep their original rules.
- End: over its last 35% of travel a hoop shortens toward its leading edge, which breaks last on the targets; the final 250 ms close every hoop and shrink the disc. No particle residue survives the area, while real sleep may persist independently.
- Colours / layers: each hoop is a psychic-ink backing, a pink or lilac stroke and a thin pale core, all alpha-blended; the disc is pink arms over pale with an ink rim. No additive white. Enemy danger keeps the last render layer.
- Upgrades: reach uses `Area.length`, width uses `Area.angle`; life / duration govern how long waves repeat. Count does not invent extra cones. End time and delayed activation use the live instance.
- Coverage target: hollow mid-tier body, ≤3% steady / ≤5% peak, height ≤1.6 m. Inspect both base and doubled reach in the bright forest and over actual danger layers; geometry checks alone are not visual acceptance.
- Cost: existing ribbon batch (at most 3 hoops × 3 strips × 37 points ≈ 670 vertices) plus one immediate sprite in the existing sprite batch. No particles, textures, materials, meshes, per-frame allocations or persistent recipe state.

Focused instance tests in `scripts/psychic-rock-vfx.test.mjs` cover live bounds, translated/interpolated origin, held and reacquired aim, forward travel, delayed activation, actual sleep attachment and cleanup.
