Signature traits
- Electricity loosed in all directions from the user's body. *[familiar — check the GIF]*

Reference game: own `nova` hook (source range not read); the generic nova (read) is a ring at the radius, a white inner ring at 0.7× and radial particles.

Sim: `nova` area on the caster, radius 4.4, one hit, life 0.3 s, cd 1.3 s, paralysis 30%.

Keep: bolts reaching out to the radius (count ≈ 2.2 × radius, min 6; every other one forks off its outer half so a 4.4 m disc stays a dense starburst; rim sparks ≈ 5 × radius), a yellow ring landing on it, small sparks. Avoid: a big white flash and additive white ground glow — first try whited out Pikachu for ~0.1 s.
